## About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Electrical and Computer Engineering at **Southern Methodist University (SMU)** in Dallas, Texas. I received my PhD in Computer Engineering from the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the **University of Tennessee, Knoxville**, where I was a **UT–ORII GATE Fellow**.

My research lies at the intersection of **machine learning, quantum machine learning, and digital health**. I explore hybrid quantum–classical models and interpretable AI for challenges in healthcare, cyber-physical systems, and wearable sensing. My work includes physiological time-series analysis, computer vision, deep learning, stress and emotion estimation, ADRD-related behavioral analytics, and anomaly detection.

I develop end-to-end research pipelines—from data acquisition and preprocessing through model development, evaluation, and deployment—using high-performance computing and emerging quantum computing tools when appropriate.

Previously, I served as a **Lecturer** in the Department of Electrical, Electronic and Communication Engineering at the **Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST)**, Bangladesh. I enjoy teaching, mentorship, interdisciplinary collaboration, and building useful open-source software.

I welcome research collaborations and conversations connecting AI, quantum computing, engineering, and healthcare.

## Featured Open-Source Projects

### ⚛️ [QuantumScope](https://github.com/Onimee58/QuantumScope)

**Interactive quantum physics visualization for Windows and Linux.** A Python/PySide6 desktop application featuring hydrogen orbital exploration, animated 2D and 3D hydrogen dynamics, and an interactive double-slit experiment. Its modern tabbed interface is designed to accommodate more quantum experiments over time.

## Featured Personal Projects

### 💰 [GlassBalance](https://github.com/Onimee58/GlassBalance)

**An offline-first personal finance tracker for Windows and Linux.** An open-source Python/PySide6 desktop application for recording income and expenses, viewing running balances and monthly history, tracking money lent or borrowed, and managing local backups. It uses a local SQLite database and does not require a cloud account.
